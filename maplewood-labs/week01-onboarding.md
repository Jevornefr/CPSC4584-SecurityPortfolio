## Maplewood Asset I Consider Highest Risk

I consider Maplewood's Electronic Health Record (EHR) system the highest-risk asset because it contains critical patient information and is directly connected to patient care. Availability and integrity are especially important because staff need accurate patient information to be available when providing treatment.

---

## My Analyst Role Takeaway

As a Tier 1 SOC analyst, my job is to monitor alerts, investigate what happened, document the facts, and escalate incidents when they require additional investigation or authority. I should not make assumptions or take actions on production systems that are outside my assigned responsibilities.

## Skills Introduced

- SOC alert monitoring and initial investigation
- Identifying critical organizational assets
- Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA) Triad
- Evidence documentation
- Incident escalation
- Separating confirmed facts from assumptions

---

## What I Learned

This onboarding activity helped me understand the responsibilities of a Tier 1 SOC analyst and why it is important to stay within my assigned role. I learned that my responsibility is not to immediately fix every security problem, but to investigate alerts, collect useful information, document what I can confirm, and escalate when a situation requires additional authority or deeper analysis.
